# Contributing

Pull requests are welcome, especially from publishers adding facts about their own apps (see the README).

## What gets merged

- Publisher files: values that can be checked against the app, its listing or its documentation.
- Fixes to wrong or misleading summaries and claims, with a link that shows what the listing says.
- Bug fixes to the site.

Every change is reviewed by the maintainer before it goes live. Pull requests that change publisher files get an automatic check within a minute: it comments with any problems and a preview of your logo. Fix what it lists, and the comment updates.
